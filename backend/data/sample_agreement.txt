RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Ramesh Kumar (the Landlord) and Anita Rao (the Tenant). Contact: ramesh@example.com, 9876543210.

1. Rent. The monthly rent shall be Rs. 20,000 payable on or before the 5th of every month.

2. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,20,000, which is 6 months of rent. The Landlord may make deductions from the deposit for any damage as deemed fit by the Landlord, and the balance will be refunded after vacation.

3. Lock-in Period. There shall be a lock-in period of 24 months. If the Tenant vacates during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall pay the remaining rent for the entire lock-in period.

4. Notice. Either party may end the tenancy by giving 2 months notice after the lock-in period.

5. Rent Increase. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time during the tenancy at its own wish.

6. Repairs. The Tenant shall bear all repairs and maintenance of the premises, including leaks and wiring.

7. Inspection. The Landlord may enter and inspect the premises at any time.

8. Termination. The Landlord may terminate this agreement immediately if the Tenant breaches any term.

9. Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet any part of the premises.
